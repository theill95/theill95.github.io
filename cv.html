Computer vision

From Pixels to SignalsHierarchical Vision Transformers for Return Prediction

With Bezirgen Veliyev and Jesper N. Wulff

I study whether modern vision models can extract return-predictive signals from price charts, and examine which parts of the chart matter for their predictions.

02
Empirical asset pricing

Decomposing Price Chart Signals in Return Prediction

With Bezirgen Veliyev and Jesper N. Wulff

A price chart and a table can represent the same market history. This paper investigates what accounts for differences between models trained on these representations.
